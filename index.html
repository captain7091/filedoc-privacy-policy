FileDoc Privacy Policy

App: FileDoc: Document Locker (com.coldregobox.filedoc)
Developer: Coldrego Box
Effective date: 7 November 2026

In short: FileDoc does not collect, upload or share any of your data. Your documents and their details stay on your phone, encrypted, and the app cannot connect to the internet.

What FileDoc stores

FileDoc stores only what you choose to add, and only on your device:

  • Documents you import (PDF, JPG and PNG files) and small preview images of them.
  • Details you enter, such as document names, categories, descriptions, tags and issue or expiry dates.
  • App settings, such as theme, layout and whether the app lock is on.

All of this is kept in FileDoc's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Documents and previews are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the database is encrypted with SQLCipher. The encryption key is protected by the Android Keystore on your device.

What FileDoc does not do

  • It has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere.
  • It has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • It does not collect personal information, contacts, location or device identifiers.
  • It does not sell or share data with anyone.
  • Android cloud backup of FileDoc's data is turned off.

Permissions and device features

  • Fingerprint, face or screen lock (USE_BIOMETRIC): used only if you turn on the app lock. Android checks your fingerprint, face, PIN or pattern; FileDoc never receives your biometric data or your PIN.
  • Camera: when you choose "Scan with camera", FileDoc opens your phone's camera app. Only the photo you take is passed back to FileDoc.
  • Files and photos: FileDoc uses Android's file and photo pickers, so it can open only the files you select. It requests no storage permission.

When data leaves FileDoc

Data leaves FileDoc only when you choose to send it:

  • Export: a copy of a document is saved to Downloads or a folder you pick. That copy is not encrypted by FileDoc.
  • Share: a copy is passed to the app you choose, such as email or a messaging app. That app's own privacy policy then applies. FileDoc removes its temporary copy shortly afterwards.
  • Backup: a backup file is saved where you choose. It is encrypted with a password you set, and FileDoc never stores that password.

Deleting your data

You can delete single documents in the app, delete everything with Settings > Clear all data, or uninstall FileDoc, which removes all of its data from your phone. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

FileDoc is a general document organiser and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
